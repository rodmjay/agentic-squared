How a step works

Your workflow asks. A person decides. The record comes back.

AI prepares everything it can. At the step only a person should do, the workflow stops and asks for one, through AgencyPro.

  1. 1AI

    Your workflow reaches the step

    Your Claude, through MCP, or your own system, through the API, gets to a step a licensed or accountable person has to sign.

  2. 2AI

    It calls request_human_step

    The request carries the kind of step, the instructions, the documents and the deadline.

  3. 3Person

    A credentialed person claims it

    A professional who holds the credential the step needs takes it and gets the prep: documents, context and the AI's draft in one place.

  4. 4Person

    Approve, reject or ask for changes

    They check the work against the source documents. AI did the prep; a person makes the call.

  5. 5Record

    The signed sign-off goes on record

    Who decided, their credential, what they decided and when, recorded and signed.

  6. 6AI

    Your workflow carries on

    The answer comes back to your workflow, which picks up where it stopped.

Why a person

AI can prepare it. It shouldn't sign it.

  • Someone accountable

    The person who signs holds the credential, and the record says who they are to your workflow.

  • Prepared before it arrives

    Documents, context and the AI's draft come together, so the professional's time goes to judgment.

  • On the record

    Every decision is recorded and signed, with who made it and when, so your workflow can trust it later.

Agentic Squared doesn't practice law, public accounting or notarization. We route each step to a licensed or qualified professional, who does the work under their own license and is responsible for it.

Questions

The short answers.

Can I send you a step today?

Not yet. Human steps aren't switched on for clients. Email us and we'll tell you when they are.

Do you give legal, tax or accounting advice?

No. We don't practice law, public accounting or notarization. Each step goes to a licensed or qualified professional who does the work under their own license.

Does the AI make the decision?

No. AI prepares the work. A person reviews it and approves, rejects or asks for changes, and that decision is what goes on record.

How does a professional get paid?

Your share shows as Pending until the client's payment fully clears: a few days for a bank transfer, 60 days for an ACH debit, about 4 months for a card. Then it's paid, for good. If the payment doesn't clear, no one is paid and no one owes anything.

What do I need on my side?

A workflow in Claude with the AgencyPro connector (local, invite-only for now), or your own system calling its REST API and webhooks. AgencyPro is in a private beta, by invitation.
